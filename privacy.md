# Workbench Recovery Privacy Policy

Workbench Recovery is a private backup utility operated for its owner's use.

## Google Drive access
The utility uses Google authorization to upload, verify, and recover backup files. It requests access only to files and folders it creates.

## Data handling
Backup contents are encrypted on the operator's computer before upload. Credentials and encryption keys are kept separately from this public website.

Google Drive stores the encrypted backups. The utility does not sell data or share it with advertisers.

## Retention and control
The operator controls backup retention and deletion. Google account permissions can be revoked at any time through Google Account settings.

## Contact
For questions, use the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.
